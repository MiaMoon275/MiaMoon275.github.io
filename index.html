About me

Curiosity became a craft.

I started programming at 14 with PureBasic, then explored C++, Java, C#, game engines, and eventually Python. That long experimental path still shapes how I work: understand the whole system, stay close to the people using it, and build only what helps.

After completing Harvard's CS50x, I focused on Django and began independently delivering software for Berlin's largest swimming club. Today my strongest work sits where backend architecture, automation, and thoughtful interfaces meet.
